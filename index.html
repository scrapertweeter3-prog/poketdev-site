Poket Dev

I run Poket Dev, an unlimited software development subscription out of Detroit. Clients pay one flat monthly fee and queue up as many requests as they want. I take them one at a time, in order, and deliver the code through a private GitHub repo.

Why one active request

Every request lives on a Trello board and moves through three columns: Requests, Active, Approved. One card sits in Active at any moment. Nothing counts as done until it lands in Approved, and revisions keep running in the same slot until you're satisfied. The queue absorbs the bursts; the single active slot keeps turnaround honest. I quote 72 hours, and onboarding takes about a day: you get your board and start filing cards.
